## Summary

A comprehensive pharmaceutical SFA application typically includes:

- **15+ Major Modules** covering all aspects of field force automation
- **100+ Features** across sales, operations, and management
- **Mobile-first design** with offline capabilities
- **Real-time analytics** and reporting
- **Comprehensive admin features** for territory and user management
- **Integration capabilities** with ERP and other systems
- **Compliance and security** features for pharmaceutical industry requirements

This feature set enables pharmaceutical companies to effectively manage their medical representatives, track sales activities, optimize territory coverage, and drive sales performance through data-driven insights.
